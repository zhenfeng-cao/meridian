Frequently asked questions

Why aren't my reminders showing up?
Make sure notifications are enabled for Meridian in your device settings, and that the per-hour reminder mode isn't set to "Off". Night hours (Zi, Chou, Yin) default to no reminder so you're not disturbed during sleep — you can change this per hour.

How do I change the reminder voice or language?
Go to Settings → Voice gender / Voice language. You can choose male or female, Chinese or English.

How does the time offset work?
If your day runs later than the clock, slide the offset forward to shift every hour's schedule to match your rhythm.

Is my data synced to the cloud?
No. Everything stays on your device. There is no account and no cloud sync.
