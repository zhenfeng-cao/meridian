Your body already has a schedule

子午流注 (zi wu liu zhu) divides the day into twelve two-hour "hours", each governed by a different organ system. Traditional Chinese medicine has timed sleep, meals, work and rest by it for two millennia. Modern science now calls the same idea circadian rhythm — and won a Nobel Prize for it in 2017.
