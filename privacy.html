Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

Meridian ("the App") is designed to keep your data private. This policy explains, in plain language, what the App does — and does not — do with your information.

Your data stays on your device

The App is fully local. Your daily plans, check-ins, compliance history, and settings are stored only on your phone, in the App's local storage. We do not collect, transmit, or store any of this data on any server.

No account, no tracking, no analytics

  • The App has no account system — you never sign up or sign in.
  • The App has no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and does not collect your device identifier (IDFA).
  • The App does not track you across apps or websites.

Permissions we use

Notifications. The App uses local notifications to deliver hour-based reminders at the times you choose. Notifications are scheduled entirely on your device; no reminder content is sent anywhere.

Photos (add only). When you choose to save your schedule poster, the App writes an image to your photo library. The App never reads or accesses your existing photos.

Health information disclaimer

The App provides general wellness information based on the traditional Chinese meridian clock (Huangdi Neijing). It is not medical advice, and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition. Consult a qualified healthcare provider for medical concerns.

Children

The App does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL.

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 8 月 24 日

Meridian（「本 App」）以保护您的隐私为设计原则。本政策用直白语言说明本 App 会做什么、不会做什么。

您的数据仅保存在本机

本 App 完全本地运行。您的每日计划、打卡、遵从度历史与设置仅保存在您手机的本地存储中。我们不会收集、传输或在任何服务器上存储这些数据。

无账号、无追踪、无分析

  • 本 App 无账号系统——无需注册或登录。
  • 本 App 无分析 SDK、无广告 SDK，不收集设备标识符（IDFA）。
  • 本 App 不追踪您在不同 App 或网站间的行为。

我们使用的权限

通知。本 App 使用本地通知，在您选择的时间发送时辰提醒。提醒完全在您设备上调度，任何提醒内容都不会发送到别处。

相册（仅写入）。当您选择保存作息海报时，本 App 会向您的相册写入一张图片。本 App 绝不会读取或访问您已有的照片。

健康信息免责声明

本 App 仅基于《黄帝内经》子午流注提供一般性养生信息，不构成医疗建议，不用于诊断、治疗、治愈或预防任何疾病。如有健康问题，请咨询专业医疗人员。

儿童

本 App 不收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童。
